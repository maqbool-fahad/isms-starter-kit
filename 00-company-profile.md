# Company Profile: Havelmetrics GmbH

## Overview
Havelmetrics GmbH is a Berlin-based B2B software company that sells a cloud
dashboard product, "StoreSight", which turns point-of-sale and online sales data
into daily performance reports for mid-sized retail chains.
Note: Havelmetrics is a fictional company created for a personal portfolio project.

## Size and structure
- Employees: 40
- Teams: engineering (15), sales and customer support (10), operations and
  finance (5), management (3), product and design (7)
- Security staff: none; one part-time IT administrator (20 hours a week)
  who handles laptops, accounts and general IT

## Technology
- Cloud platform: Microsoft Azure (production and test environments)
- Product database: Azure SQL, holds retail customers' sales data and
  some personal data (customer contact details, user accounts)
- Source code: GitHub (private repositories)
- Email, files and chat: Microsoft 365 with Microsoft Entra ID for sign-in
- Devices: company-issued laptops for all staff; around 12 employees also
  read work email on personal phones
- Third-party suppliers: payment provider, customer support tool,
  cloud hosting backup service

## Working model
- Hybrid: two office days a week in Berlin (Kreuzberg), the rest remote
- Staff sometimes work from home networks and cafes

## Customers and data
- Customers: mid-sized retail chains in Germany and the EU (about 35 companies)
- Data handled: sales data, store performance metrics, customer contact
  details, staff user accounts
- Personal data is processed, so GDPR applies. The company may also fall
  under NIS2 as a supplier to larger organisations.

## Why an ISMS now
- Two enterprise customers have said they will sign only if Havelmetrics
  holds ISO 27001 certification
- Security questionnaires from customers are increasing and are answered
  informally today
- The founders want a structured approach, not ad hoc fixes

## Constraints
- Small budget, no dedicated security team
- Target: ready for a certification audit within 12 months
- Some existing controls (MFA on email, laptop disk encryption) exist
  but are not documented or consistently applied
