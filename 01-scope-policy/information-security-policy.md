# Information Security Policy: Havelmetrics GmbH

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how Havelmetrics GmbH protects the information it
holds, especially the sales data and user accounts that customers entrust
to StoreSight. It supports our legal duties under GDPR, our contracts with
customers, and our goal of ISO 27001 certification.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to everything covered by the ISMS scope statement
(see isms-scope.md) and to all employees, contractors and suppliers who
access Havelmetrics systems or data.

## 3. Objectives
- All staff accounts use multi-factor authentication within 3 months.
  Phishing-resistant methods (passkeys or hardware security keys) are
  required for administrator accounts and preferred for all staff.
- No critical risk stays open for longer than 30 days.
- Every employee completes security awareness training once a year.
- Security incidents are reported and logged within 24 hours of discovery.
- Access for leavers is removed on their last working day.

## 4. Principles
- Confidentiality: only authorised people can see customer and company data.
- Integrity: data stays accurate and is not changed without authorisation.
- Availability: StoreSight and its data are available when customers need them.

## 5. Roles and responsibilities
- Management (CEO and founders): approve this policy, provide budget and
  review ISMS performance at least once a year.
- ISMS Manager: runs the ISMS day to day, maintains the risk register and
  reports to management. At present this role is held by the part-time
  IT administrator.
- All staff: follow security rules, complete annual training and report
  incidents or suspicious activity immediately.

## 6. Commitments
Havelmetrics commits to comply with GDPR, customer contracts and other
applicable legal requirements, to improve the ISMS continuously, and to
provide the people, time and tools it needs.
Biometric unlock is used only on the user's own device and is never
collected or stored by Havelmetrics.

## 7. Review
This policy is reviewed once a year, and after any major security incident
or significant change to the business or its systems.
