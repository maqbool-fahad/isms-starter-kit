# Risk Assessment Method: Havelmetrics GmbH

## 1. Purpose
This document explains how Havelmetrics identifies, scores and treats
information security risks, so that risks are assessed consistently and
decisions can be explained and repeated.

## 2. Approach
1. Identify assets (systems, data, people, suppliers).
2. Identify threats and vulnerabilities for each asset.
3. Score likelihood and impact.
4. Calculate the risk score and level.
5. Choose a treatment and assign an owner and due date.
6. Review the risk register regularly.

## 3. Likelihood scale
| Score | Level | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Not expected in the next 3 years |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could happen once in 1 to 3 years |
| 3 | Possible | Could happen once a year |
| 4 | Likely | Expected several times a year |
| 5 | Almost certain | Happening now or expected monthly |

## 4. Impact scale
| Score | Level | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor | Little disruption, no customer data affected |
| 2 | Moderate | Short disruption, limited internal data affected |
| 3 | Significant | Customer-visible disruption, contract concerns |
| 4 | Major | Customer data exposed, GDPR reporting likely |
| 5 | Severe | Large breach, loss of key customers, regulatory action |

## 5. Risk score and levels
Risk score = likelihood x impact (1 to 25).

| Score | Level | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | Low | Accept and monitor |
| 5 to 9 | Medium | Treat within 6 months |
| 10 to 14 | High | Treat within 90 days |
| 15 to 25 | Critical | Treat within 30 days; management informed immediately |

## 6. Treatment options
- Reduce: add or improve controls (most common)
- Avoid: stop the activity that causes the risk
- Transfer: share the risk, for example through insurance or contracts
- Accept: formally agree to live with the risk

## 7. Risk acceptance
- Low and Medium risks can be accepted by the ISMS Manager.
- High and Critical risks can be accepted only by management, in writing,
  with a reason and a review date.

## 8. Residual risk
After treatment, the risk is scored again. The remaining score is the
residual risk and must fall within an acceptable level (Medium or lower).

## 9. Review
The risk register is reviewed every quarter, after major incidents, and
when systems, suppliers or laws change.
